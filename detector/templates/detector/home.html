How It Works

Analyze a suspicious bank email in three simple steps using BankGuard's rule-based NLP detection process.

1
Paste Email

Enter or paste the suspicious bank email into the BankGuard detector.

2
Analyze

The rule-based NLP engine checks keywords, urgency, sensitive information requests, banking indicators and URLs.

3
Get Risk Result

The system generates a risk score and classifies the email as LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH.
