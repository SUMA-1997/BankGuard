Understanding Risk Levels

High Risk — Be Extremely Careful

The analysis found strong suspicious indicators. Do not click links or provide confidential information. Verify the message using your bank's official channels.

Medium Risk — Check Before Acting

The message contains some indicators that need review. Examine the sender, website address, wording, and unexpected requests.

Low Risk — Stay Alert

Fewer suspicious indicators were detected. This does not guarantee the email is genuine. Continue checking the sender and links.

What Should You Do?

1

Check the sender. Verify the sender's email address carefully.

2

Inspect links. Avoid unexpected links and unfamiliar websites.

3

Protect your details. Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV, or password in response to an email.

4

Verify independently. Contact your bank using its official website or customer-support number.
